Privacy Policy

Canadian Pay Cheque Calculator · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Canadian Pay Cheque Calculator (the “App”) is developed and operated by Ali Sharabiani (“I”, “me”). The App is free and supported by ads, with an optional one-time purchase to remove them. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the App, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The income, province and other values you enter stay on your device. I never receive them.
  • The App doesn't ask for your name, email address, or any account.
  • Google services in the App (AdMob ads, Firebase Analytics and Google Play Billing) collect limited device and usage information as described below.
  • I don't sell your personal information.

Information you enter

Your income, pay period, hours per week, province and tax year are used only to calculate results on your device. They're saved locally so the App remembers your choices, and they may be included in your device's Android backup if you've turned that on. They aren't sent to me or to any third party.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and your ad choices

  • In regions where consent is required (for example the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the App asks for your choice using Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown.
  • You can change that choice at any time from the App's menu under Ad privacy choices, when it applies to your region.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact location varies by device).
  • Buying Remove ads turns ads off. The App then no longer loads the Google Mobile Ads SDK or shows a consent message.

Analytics (Firebase)

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and to improve it. This includes information such as app opens, the screens you view, the app version, device model, operating system, country, and an app instance identifier. It doesn't include the amounts you enter. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

In-app purchase (Google Play)

The optional Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. I never receive your payment card details. The App gets confirmation of the purchase from Google Play and saves on your device that ads are removed, so the purchase can be restored when you reinstall the App or use a new device signed in to the same Google account. Google provides me with order records, such as the order number, date, price and country, for accounting and refunds.

Sharing of information

Information is shared only with the Google services described above, which act under their own privacy policies. I don't sell or rent personal information. I may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Values you enter remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google services is kept according to Google's retention policies. To ask about or request deletion of any information I hold, such as purchase records, contact me at the address below.

Children

The App is intended for adults and isn't directed to children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I'll take appropriate action.

Security and international transfers

Information sent by Google services is encrypted in transit. Google may process information on servers outside Canada, including in the United States. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Canada's PIPEDA or the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to withdraw consent. Contact me to exercise these rights.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date and take effect when posted.

Contact
